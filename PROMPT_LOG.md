# AI Prompt Log

## 1. Exact Prompt

I am a second-year Computer Information Systems student creating a one-page semantic HTML5 profile and mini-portfolio for my Web Technologies course.

Write a short first-person About Me section for my portfolio.

### Goal

Create a natural introduction that presents me as a student interested in technology, software development, web development, information systems, and innovation.

### Constraints

* 80–120 words.
* Write in the first person using "I" and "my".
* Use a professional but friendly tone.
* Make it suitable for a university student's portfolio.
* Do not exaggerate my experience or claim that I am an expert.
* Avoid overly generic phrases such as "passionate about changing the world."
* Mention that I am developing my technical and problem-solving skills through practical projects.
* Do not use bullet points.

### Format

Return only the About Me paragraph.

## 2. Raw AI Output

Hi! I'm a second-year Computer Information Systems student with a genuine interest in technology, software development, and the way well-designed systems solve real problems. Web development has become a particular focus for me, since it combines creativity with logic in a way I find satisfying to work through. I'm currently developing my technical and problem-solving skills through practical, hands-on projects, learning by building rather than just studying theory. I'm also drawn to innovation, especially how new tools and approaches reshape the way people interact with information. While I'm still early in my studies, I approach every project as a chance to learn something concrete and grow as a developer.

## 3. Final Edited Version

I am a Computer Information Systems student who enjoys learning how technology can be used to create practical solutions. I am developing my skills in web development, programming, software development and information systems through my university studies and personal projects. I especially enjoy building digital projects and improving them as I learn new technologies. I am still growing my technical and problem-solving skills, but I enjoy challenging myself, learning from mistakes and turning ideas into working solutions.

## 4. Reflection

I used the AI-generated response as a starting point for my About section. I edited the wording to make it sound more natural and to ensure that it accurately represents my interests and current level of experience. I also removed or changed any statements that sounded too general or exaggerated.
